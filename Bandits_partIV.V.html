    Minimax and Instance-Dependent Lower Bounds

    Proof of Theorem 15.2 - Minimax lower bounds

  • Regret Decomposition: \(R_n(\pi, \nu_\mu) = \Delta \cdot (n - \mathbb{E}_\mu[T_1(n)]) \geq \mathbb{P}_\mu(T_1(n) \leq n/2) \cdot \frac{n\Delta}{2}\), and \(R_n(\pi, \nu_{\mu'}) > \mathbb{P}_{\mu'}(T_1(n) > n/2) \cdot \frac{n\Delta}{2}\).
  • Apply Bratagnolle-Huber Inequality: \[{R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{\mu }}\right) + {R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{{\mu }^{\prime }}}\right) > \frac{n\Delta }{2}\left( {{\mathbb{P}}_{\mu }\left( {{T}_{1}\left( n\right) \leq n/2}\right) + {\mathbb{P}}_{{\mu }^{\prime }}\left( {{T}_{1}\left( n\right) > n/2}\right) }\right) \tag{15.3}\] \[\geq \frac{n\Delta }{4}\exp \left( {-\mathrm{D}\left( {{\mathbb{P}}_{\mu },{\mathbb{P}}_{{\mu }^{\prime }}}\right) }\right) .\]
  • Apply Divergence Decomposition: \[\mathrm{D}\left( {{\mathbb{P}}_{\mu },{\mathbb{P}}_{{\mu }^{\prime }}}\right) = {\mathbb{E}}_{\mu }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( n\right) }\right\rbrack \mathrm{D}\left( {\mathcal{N}\left( {0,1}\right) ,\mathcal{N}\left( {{2\Delta },1}\right) }\right) = {\mathbb{E}}_{\mu }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( n\right) }\right\rbrack \frac{{\left( 2\Delta \right) }^{2}}{2} \leq \frac{{2n}{\Delta }^{2}}{k - 1}.\]
  • Finally \[2{R}_{n}^{ * }\left( {{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{N}}^{k}\left( 1\right) }\right)=2\max\{ {R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{\mu }}\right) , {R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{{\mu }^{\prime }}}\right) \}\geq{R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{\mu }}\right) + {R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,{\nu }_{{\mu }^{\prime }}}\right) \geq \frac{n\Delta }{4}\exp \left( {-\frac{{2n}{\Delta }^{2}}{k - 1}}\right) .\] completed by choosing \(\Delta = \sqrt{\left( {k - 1}\right) /{4n}} \leq 1/2\) . Hence,\[{R}_{n}^{ * }\left( {{\mathcal{E}}_{\mathcal{N}}^{k}\left( 1\right) }\right) \geq \frac{1}{27}\sqrt{\left( {k - 1}\right) n}.\]

    Minimax Lower Bounds of Stochastic Linear Bandits

    Proof of Theorem 24.1

  • Divergence Decomposition for Gaussian, for More General Action Space: \[\mathrm{D}\left( {{\mathbb{P}}_{\theta },{\mathbb{P}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}}\right) = {\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathrm{D}\left( {\mathcal{N}\left( {\left\langle {{A}_{t},\theta }\right\rangle ,1}\right) ,\mathcal{N}\left( {\left\langle {{A}_{t},{\theta }^{\prime }}\right\rangle ,1}\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack\] \[= \frac{1}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\left\langle {A}_{t},\theta - {\theta }^{\prime }\right\rangle }^{2}\right\rbrack . \tag{24.1}\]
  • Apply Bratagnolle-Huber Inequality: Define \[{p}_{\theta i} = {\mathbb{P}}_{\theta }\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {\operatorname{sign}\left( {A}_{ti}\right) \neq \operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) }\right\} \geq n/2}\right) .\] Let \({\theta }_{i}^{\prime } = - {\theta }_{i}\), and \({\theta }_{j}^{\prime } = {\theta }_{j}\) when \(j\neq i\), then \[{p}_{\theta i} + {p}_{{\theta }^{\prime }i} \geq \frac{1}{2}\exp \left( {-\frac{1}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\left\langle {A}_{t},\theta - {\theta }^{\prime }\right\rangle }^{2}\right\rbrack }\right) \geq \frac{1}{2}\exp \left( {-2}\right) . \tag{24.2}\] Averaging over \(\Theta\), \[\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\theta \in \Theta }}\frac{1}{\left| \Theta \right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{p}_{\theta i} = \frac{1}{\left| \Theta \right| }\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\theta \in \Theta }}{p}_{\theta i} \geq \frac{d}{4}\exp \left( {-2}\right) .\] 由于LHS是均值，所以均值对象肯定有一个\(\geq RHS\), 否则没有一个大于右式的话均值不可能大于右式。因此存在\(\theta \in \Theta\) 使得 \[\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{p}_{\theta i} \geq d\exp \left( {-2}\right) /4\]
  • Rewrite Regret: \[{R}_{n}\left( {\mathcal{A},\theta }\right) = {\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\left( {a_i^*- {A}_{ti}}\right) {\theta }_{i}}\right\rbrack\] \[= {\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\left( {\operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) - {A}_{ti}}\right) {\theta }_{i}}\right\rbrack\] \[\geq \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {\operatorname{sign}\left( {A}_{ti}\right) \neq \operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) }\right\} }\right\rbrack\] (case-based analysis for the sign of \(\theta\) and \(A_{ti}\)) \[\geq \frac{\sqrt{n}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{\mathbb{P}}_{\theta }\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {\operatorname{sign}\left( {A}_{ti}\right) \neq \operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) }\right\} \geq n/2}\right)\] (Markov Inequality) \[= \frac{\sqrt{n}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{p}_{\theta i} \geq \frac{\exp \left( {-2}\right) }{8}d\sqrt{n},\] (holds with a certain \(\theta\))

    Minimax Lower Bounds of Stochastic Linear Bandits

    Proof of Theorem 24.2

  • Let \(\Delta = \frac{1}{4\sqrt{3}}\sqrt{d/n}\) and \(\theta \in \{ \pm \Delta {\} }^{d}\) and define \({\tau }_{i} = n \land \min \{ t: \left. {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{s = 1}}^{t}{A}_{si}^{2} \geq n/d}\right\}\)
  • Regret Decomposition: \[ \begin{aligned} R_n &= \mathbb{E}_\theta \left[ \sum_{t=1}^n \sum_{i=1}^d \theta_i (a_i^* - A_{ti}) \right] \\ &= \mathbb{E}_\theta \left[ \sum_{t=1}^n \sum_{i=1}^d \theta_i \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} \operatorname{sign}(\theta_i) - A_{ti} \right) \right] \\ &= \Delta \, \mathbb{E}_\theta \left[ \sum_{t=1}^n \sum_{i=1}^d \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} - A_{ti} \operatorname{sign}(\theta_i) \right) \right] \end{aligned}\] \[\geq \frac{\Delta \sqrt{d}}{2}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} - {A}_{ti}\operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) \right) }^{2}}\right\rbrack\] \[\geq \frac{\Delta \sqrt{d}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} - {A}_{ti}\operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) \right) }^{2}}\right\rbrack ,\]
  • Applying Pinsker's Inequality: let \({U}_{i}\left( x\right) = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{\left( 1/\sqrt{d} - {A}_{ti}x\right) }^{2}\), because \[{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{\left( 1/\sqrt{d} - {A}_{ti}\right) }^{2} \leq 2\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}\frac{1}{d} + 2\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{A}_{ti}^{2} \leq \frac{4n}{d} + 2,\] then \[{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta ^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack -{\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack\leq \frac{4n}{d} + 2\] \[{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack \geq {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack - \left( {\frac{4n}{d} + 2}\right) \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{D}\left( {\mathbb{P},{\mathbb{P}}^{\prime }}\right) }\]
  • KL (Relative) Entropy for Gausssian Distribution: $$ \mathrm{D}(P_i, P_i')=D\left( \mathcal{N}(\mu, 1) \parallel \mathcal{N}(\mu', 1) \right) = \frac{1}{2} (\mu - \mu')^2 = \frac{1}{2} \Delta^2 $$
  • Applying Stopping-Time Version of Divergence Decompostion: \[ {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack \geq {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack - \frac{\Delta }{2}\left( {\frac{4n}{d} + 2}\right) \sqrt{\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{A}_{ti}^{2}}\right\rbrack } \tag{24.3}\] \[\geq {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack - \frac{\Delta }{2}\left( {\frac{4n}{d} + 2}\right) \sqrt{\frac{n}{d} + 1} \tag{24.4}\] (by definition) \[\geq {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack - \frac{4\sqrt{3}{\Delta n}}{d}\sqrt{\frac{n}{d}}, \tag{24.5}\] (by assumption that \(d\leq2n\))
  • Therefore, \[{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) }\right\rbrack + {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( {-1}\right) }\right\rbrack \geq {\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( 1\right) + {U}_{i}\left( {-1}\right) }\right\rbrack - \frac{4\sqrt{3}{n\Delta }}{d}\sqrt{\frac{n}{d}}\] \[= 2{\mathbb{E}}_{{\theta }^{\prime }}\left\lbrack {\frac{{\tau }_{i}}{d} + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{{\tau }_{i}}{A}_{ti}^{2}}\right\rbrack - \frac{4\sqrt{3}{n\Delta }}{d}\sqrt{\frac{n}{d}} \geq \frac{2n}{d} - \frac{4\sqrt{3}{n\Delta }}{d}\sqrt{\frac{n}{d}} = \frac{n}{d}.\]
  • Finally using randomization hammer, \[\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\theta \in \{ \pm \Delta {\} }^{d}}}{R}_{n}\left( {\mathcal{A},\theta }\right) \geq \frac{\Delta \sqrt{d}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{\theta \in \{ \pm \Delta {\} }^{d}}}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( {\operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack\] \[= \frac{\Delta \sqrt{d}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{\theta }_{-i} \in \{ \pm \Delta {\} }^{d - 1}}}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{\theta }_{i} \in \{ \pm \Delta \} }}{\mathbb{E}}_{\theta }\left\lbrack {{U}_{i}\left( {\operatorname{sign}\left( {\theta }_{i}\right) }\right) }\right\rbrack\] \[\geq \frac{\Delta \sqrt{d}}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{d}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{{\theta }_{-i} \in \{ \pm \Delta {\} }^{d - 1}}}\frac{n}{d} = {2}^{d - 2}{n\Delta }\sqrt{d}.\]
  • hence \[{R}_{n}\left( {\mathcal{A},\theta }\right) \geq \frac{{n\Delta }\sqrt{d}}{4} = \frac{d\sqrt{n}}{{16}\sqrt{3}}\]
