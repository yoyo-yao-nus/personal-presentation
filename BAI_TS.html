Simple Regret

  • \[{R}_{n}^{\mathrm{{SIMPLE}}}\left( {\pi ,\nu }\right) = {\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{\Delta }_{{A}_{n + 1}}\left( \nu \right) }\right\rbrack .\]

    • Uniform Exploration

  • Explore each arm in the first \(n\) rounds and select the empirically best arm in round \(n+1\).

    • Theorem 33.1 - UE Regret Bound

  • Let \(\nu \in {\mathcal{E}}_{SG}^{k}\left( 1\right)\) be a 1-subgaussian bandit, then for all \(n\geq k\), \[{R}_{n}^{\text{SIMPLE }}\left( {\pi ,\nu }\right) \leq \mathop{\min }\limits_{{\Delta \geq 0}}\left( {\Delta + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i : {\Delta }_{i}\left( \nu \right) > \Delta }}{\Delta }_{i}\left( \nu \right) \exp \left( {-\frac{\lfloor n/k\rfloor {\Delta }_{i}{\left( \nu \right) }^{2}}{4}}\right) }\right) .\]

    • Proof of Theorem 33.1

  • Markov inequality: Since \({A}_{n + 1} = i\) implies that \({\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( n\right) \geq {\widehat{\mu }}_{1}\left( n\right)\), and \({T}_{i}\left( n\right) \geq \lfloor n/k\rfloor\) is not random, \[\mathbb{P}\left( {{\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( n\right) \geq {\widehat{\mu }}_{1}\left( n\right) }\right) = \mathbb{P}\left( {{\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( n\right) - {\widehat{\mu }}_{1}\left( n\right) \geq 0}\right) \leq \exp \left( {-\frac{\lfloor n/k\rfloor {\Delta }_{i}^{2}}{4}}\right) . \tag{33.1}\]
  • Simple Regret Decomposition: \[{R}_{n}^{\text{SIMPLE}}\left( {\pi ,\nu }\right) = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\Delta }_{i}\mathbb{P}\left( {{A}_{n + 1} = i}\right) \leq \Delta + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i : {\Delta }_{i} > \Delta }}{\Delta }_{i}\mathbb{P}\left( {{A}_{n + 1} = i}\right) .\]
  • Finally, take the minimum over all \(\Delta\geq 0\).

    • Proposition 33.2 Relation between Simple Regret and Cumulative Regret

  • Define \[{\pi }_{n + 1}\left( {i \mid {a}_{1},{x}_{1},\ldots ,{a}_{n},{x}_{n}}\right) = \frac{1}{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {{a}_{t} = i}\right\} .\], then \[{R}_{n}^{SIMPLE}\left( {{\left( {\pi }_{t}\right) }_{t = 1}^{n + 1},\nu }\right) = \frac{{R}_{n}\left( {\pi ,\nu }\right) }{n},\]

    • Corollary 33.3 Minimax Simple Regret

  • There exist a constant \( 0 < C \) such that for all \( n,k \geq 1 \) , \[\mathop{\inf }\limits_{\pi }\mathop{\sup }\limits_{\substack{{\nu \in {\mathcal{E}}_{SG}^{k}\left( 1\right) : } \\ {\Delta \left( \nu \right) \in {\left\lbrack 0,1\right\rbrack }^{k}} }}{R}_{n}^{{}^{SIMPLE}}\left( {\pi ,\nu }\right) \leq C\sqrt{k/n}.\]
  • May lead to a highly suboptimal policy.

    Pure Exploration - BAI with a Fixed Confidence

    Definition 33.4 Soundness of \(\pi,\tau,\psi\)

  • A triple \( \left( {\pi ,\tau ,\psi }\right) \) is sound at confidence level \( \delta \in \left( {0,1}\right) \) for environment class \( \mathcal{E} \) if for all \( \nu \in \mathcal{E} \) \[{\mathbb{P}}_{\nu \pi }\left( {\tau < \infty \text{ and }{\Delta }_{\psi }\left( \nu \right) > 0}\right) \leq \delta . \tag{33.3}\]

    • Theorem 33.5 Lower Bound of the Stopping Time

  • Assume that \( \left( {\pi ,\tau ,\psi }\right) \) is sound for \( \mathcal{E} \) at confidence level \( \delta \in \left( {0,1}\right) \) , and let \( \nu \in \mathcal{E} \) . Then \( {\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack \geq {c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) \log \left( \frac{1}{4\delta }\right) \) ,where \[{c}^{ * }{\left( \nu \right) }^{-1} = \mathop{\sup }\limits_{{\alpha \in {\mathcal{P}}_{k - 1}}}\left( {\mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{alt}\left( \nu \right) }}\left( {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\alpha }_{i}\mathrm{\;D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) }\right) }\right) \tag{33.4}\]
  • Interpretation of \({c}^{ * }{\left( \nu \right) }^{-1}\):

    • Proof of Theorem 33.5

  • Define event \( E = \left\{ {\tau < \infty \text{and}\psi \notin {i}^{ * }\left( {\nu }^{\prime }\right) }\right\} \in {\mathcal{F}}_{\tau } \) then, \[{2\delta } \geq {\mathbb{P}}_{\nu \pi }\left( {\tau < \infty \text{ and }\psi \notin {i}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) }\right) + {\mathbb{P}}_{{\nu }^{\prime }\pi }\left( {\tau < \infty \text{ and }\psi \notin {i}^{ * }\left( {\nu }^{\prime }\right) }\right) \] (soundness of \((\pi,\tau,\psi)\)) \[ \geq {\mathbb{P}}_{\nu \pi }\left( {E}^{c}\right) + {\mathbb{P}}_{{\nu }^{\prime }\pi }\left( E\right) \] (because \( {\mathbb{P}}_{\nu \pi }\left( {\tau = \infty }\right) = 0 \) and \( {i}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) \cap {i}^{ * }\left( {\nu }^{\prime }\right) = \varnothing \)) \[ \geq \frac{1}{2}\exp \left( {-\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) }\right\rbrack \mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) }\right) . \tag{33.5}\] (Bretagnolle-Huber inequality and the stopping time version of Lemma 15.1)
  • Hence, \[\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) }\right\rbrack \mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \geq \log \left( \frac{1}{4\delta }\right) , \tag{33.6}\]
  • Using the definition of \({c}^{ * }{\left( \nu \right) }^{-1}\), \[\frac{{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack }{{c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) } = {\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack \mathop{\sup }\limits_{{\alpha \in {\mathcal{P}}_{k - 1}}}\mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( \nu \right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\alpha }_{i}\mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \] \[ \geq {\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack \mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( \nu \right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\frac{{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) }\right\rbrack }{{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack }\mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \tag{33.7}\] (the upper bound of \(\alpha_i\geq \alpha^*(v)\)) \[ = \mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( \nu \right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) }\right\rbrack \mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \] \[ \geq \log \left( \frac{1}{4\delta }\right).\]

    Track-and-Stop Algorithm

  • Track the optimal distribution of arms and force explorations: play the arm for which \( t{\widehat{\alpha }}_{i}\left( t\right) - {T}_{i}\left( t\right) \) is maximised
  • Stop when we are confidence enough to recommend the optimal arm: if \( \tau = \min \left\{ {t : {Z}_{t} > }\right. \) \( \left. {{\beta }_{t}\left( \delta \right) }\right\} \) ,then the empirically optimal arm at time \( \tau \) is the best arm with probability at least \( 1 - \delta \)

    • Theorem 33.6

  • There exists a choice of \( {\beta }_{t}\left( \delta \right) \) such that track-and-stop is sound and for all \( \nu \in \mathcal{E} \) with \( \left| {{i}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) }\right| = 1 \) it holds that \[\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{\delta \rightarrow 0}}\frac{{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack }{\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) } = {c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) .\]

    • Lemma 33.7

  • for \( \tau = \min \left\{ {t : {Z}_{t} \geq {\beta }_{t}\left( \delta \right) }\right\} \) ,it holds that \[ \mathbb{P}\left( {{i}^{ * }\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( \tau \right) }\right) \neq {i}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) }\right) \leq \delta . \]

    • Proof of Lemma 33.7

  • We know from the proof of Theorem 33.5 that \[\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) }\right\rbrack \mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \geq \log \left( \frac{1}{4\delta }\right) , \tag{33.6}\] Then a reasonable stopping time may be estimated from \[\mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( \nu \right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{T}_{i}\left( t\right) \mathrm{D}\left( {{\nu }_{i},{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) \gtrsim \log \left( \frac{1}{\delta }\right) .\] Under the Subgaussian Bandit Environment, we have \[{Z}_{t} = \mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\nu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( t\right) }\right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{T}_{i}\left( t\right) \mathrm{D}\left( {{\widehat{\nu }}_{i}\left( t\right) ,{\nu }_{i}^{\prime }}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\mathop{\inf }\limits_{{{\mu }^{\prime } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( {\widehat{\mu }\left( t\right) }\right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{T}_{i}\left( t\right) {\left( {\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( t\right) - {\mu }_{i}\left( {\nu }^{\prime }\right) \right) }^{2}.\]
  • Because \[\left\{ {\nu \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( \tau \right) }\right) }\right\} \subseteq \left\{ {\frac{1}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) {\left( {\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( \tau \right) - {\mu }_{i}\right) }^{2} \geq {\beta }_{\tau }\left( \delta \right) }\right\} ,\]we have \[\mathbb{P}\left( {1 \notin {i}^{ * }\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( \tau \right) }\right) }\right) = \mathbb{P}\left( {\nu \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( \tau \right) }\right) }\right) \leq \mathbb{P}\left( {\frac{1}{2}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{T}_{i}\left( \tau \right) {\left( {\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( \tau \right) - {\mu }_{i}\right) }^{2} \geq {\beta }_{\tau }\left( \delta \right) }\right) \] \[<\delta\] (Using concentration from Lemma 33.8 and Proposition 33.9)

    • Proof of Theorem 33.6

  • As we expect minimization of \((t\alpha_i^*(v)-T_i(t))\), we expect \[{Z}_{t} = \mathop{\inf }\limits_{{\widetilde{\nu } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( {\widehat{\nu }\left( t\right) }\right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\frac{{T}_{i}\left( t\right) {\left( {\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( t\right) - {\mu }_{i}\left( \widetilde{\nu }\right) \right) }^{2}}{2}\] \[ \approx t\mathop{\inf }\limits_{{\widetilde{\nu } \in {\mathcal{E}}_{\text{alt }}\left( \nu \right) }}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\frac{{\alpha }_{i}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) {\left( {\mu }_{i}\left( \nu \right) - {\mu }_{i}\left( \widetilde{\nu }\right) \right) }^{2}}{2}\] \[ = \frac{t}{{c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) }\text{.}\]
  • Given Lemma 33.7, the algorithm should halt when \[\frac{t}{{c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) } \geq {\beta }_{t}\left( \delta \right) = \left( {1 + o\left( 1\right) }\right) \log \left( {1/\delta }\right) ,\] which occurs once \( t \geq \left( {1 + o\left( 1\right) }\right) {c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) \log \left( {1/\delta }\right) \) .
  • Hence, \[\mathop{\lim }\limits_{{\delta \rightarrow 0}}\frac{{\mathbb{E}}_{\nu \pi }\left\lbrack \tau \right\rbrack }{\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) } = {c}^{ * }\left( \nu \right) .\]

    Pure Exploration - BAI with a Budget

    Sequential Halving Algorithm

  • Divides the budget into \( L = \left\lceil {{\log }_{2}\left( k\right) }\right\rceil \) phases. In the first phase,the algorithm chooses each arm equally often. The bottom half of the arms are then eliminated, and the process is repeated.
  • More efficient than Uniform Exploration when suboptimality gaps are not similar to each other.

    • Theorem 33.10 Upper Bound of the Suboptimal Probability

  • If \( {\mu }_{1} \geq \cdots \geq {\mu }_{k} \) and \( \pi \) is sequential halving, then \[{\mathbb{P}}_{\nu \pi }\left( {{\Delta }_{{A}_{n + 1}} > 0}\right) \leq 3{\log }_{2}\left( k\right) \exp \left( {-\frac{n}{{16}{H}_{2}\left( \mu \right) {\log }_{2}\left( k\right) }}\right) ,\] where \( {H}_{2}\left( \mu \right) = \mathop{\max }\limits_{{i : {\Delta }_{i} > 0}}\frac{i}{{\Delta }_{i}^{2}} \) .

    • Proof of Theorem 33.10

  • Let \[\operatorname{TOPM}\left( {A,m}\right) = \left\{ {i \in \left\lbrack k\right\rbrack : \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{j \leq i}}\mathbb{I}\{ j \in A\} \leq m}\right\}. \]
  • Prove that \(|A_{L+1}| = 1\): because \(L = \log_2(k)\) and \(|A_\ell| = k \cdot 2^{1-\ell}\), \[ |A_{L+1}| = k \cdot 2^{1-(L+1)} = k \cdot 2^{-L} = k \cdot \frac{1}{k} = 1. \]
  • Use Markov Inequality to prove that \[\mathbb{P} \left( \hat{\mu}_1^\ell \leq \hat{\mu}_i^\ell \mid i \in A_\ell, 1 \in A_\ell \right) \leq \exp \left( -\frac{T_\ell \Delta_i^2}{4} \right)\].
  • Let \( {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }^{\prime } = {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell } \smallsetminus \operatorname{TOPM}\left( {{\mathcal{A}}_{\ell },\left\lceil {\left| {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }\right| /4}\right\rceil }\right) \) be the bottom three-quarters of the arms in round \( \ell \) .
  • Then if the optimal arm is eliminated after the \( \ell \) th phase, then because \(|\mathcal{A}_\ell'| = \frac{3}{4} |A_\ell|\), and at least \(|A_\ell|/2\) arms satisfy \(\hat{\mu}_i^\ell \geq \hat{\mu}_1^\ell\),
  • and even all arms in \(B = \operatorname{TOPM}\left( {{\mathcal{A}}_{\ell },\left\lceil {\left| {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }\right| /4}\right\rceil }\right)\) satisfy \(\hat{\mu}_i^\ell \geq \hat{\mu}_1^\ell\), then
  • \[{N}_{\ell } = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i \in {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }^{\prime }}}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {{\widehat{\mu }}_{i}^{\ell } \geq {\widehat{\mu }}_{1}^{\ell }}\right\} \geq |A_\ell|/2 - |B| = |A_\ell|/4 = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} |A_\ell| = \frac{1}{3}\left| {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }^{\prime }\right| .\] Hence, \[ \mathbb{P} \left( 1 \notin A_{\ell+1} \mid A_\ell \right) \leq \mathbb{P} \left( N_\ell \geq \frac{1}{3} |\mathcal{A}_\ell'| \mid A_\ell \right). \] Due to Markov Inequality, \[ \mathbb{P} \left( N_\ell \geq \frac{1}{3} |\mathcal{A}_\ell'| \mid A_\ell \right) \leq \frac{\mathbb{E}[N_\ell \mid A_\ell]}{\frac{1}{3} |\mathcal{A}_\ell'|} \leq \frac{3 \mathbb{E}[N_\ell \mid A_\ell]}{|\mathcal{A}_\ell'|}. \]
  • Let \( {i}_{\ell } = \min {\mathcal{A}}_{\ell }^{\prime } \) be the minimum index of the set, which means the corresponding mean reward is the maximum, and \(i_\ell \geq |A_\ell|/4 + 1\geq \frac{k 2^{1-\ell}}{4} \implies 2^{\ell-1} i_\ell \geq 2^{\ell-1} \cdot \frac{k 2^{1-\ell}}{4} = \frac{k}{4} \geq \frac{1}{4}\), then \[ \mathbb{E}[N_\ell | A_\ell] = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}_\ell'} \mathbb{P} \left( \hat{\mu}_i^\ell \geq \hat{\mu}_1^\ell \mid A_\ell \right) \leq \sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}_\ell'} \exp \left( -\frac{T_\ell \Delta_i^2}{4} \right) \leq |\mathcal{A}_\ell'| \max_{i \in \mathcal{A}_\ell'} \exp \left( -\frac{T_\ell \Delta_i^2}{4} \right) \] \[ = |\mathcal{A}_\ell'|\exp \left( -\min_{i \in \mathcal{A}_\ell'} \frac{T_\ell \Delta_i^2}{4} \right)=|\mathcal{A}_\ell'|\exp\left( -\frac{T_\ell \Delta_{i_\ell}^2}{4}\right) \] \[ =|\mathcal{A}_\ell'|\exp\left( -\frac{n 2^{\ell-1} \Delta_{i_\ell}^2}{4\log_2(k)}\right) \leq |\mathcal{A}_\ell'|\exp\left(-\frac{n \Delta_{i_\ell}^2}{16 i_\ell \log_2(k)} \right) \]
  • Hence, \[ \mathbb{P} \left( 1 \notin A_{\ell+1} \mid 1 \in A_\ell \right) \leq 3 \exp \left( -\frac{n \Delta_{i_\ell}^2}{16 i_\ell \log_2(k)} \right). \]
  • Finally, \[ \mathbb{P} \left( 1 \notin A_{L+1} \right) \leq \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} \mathbb{P} \left( 1 \notin A_{\ell+1} \mid 1 \in A_\ell \right) \leq \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} 3 \exp \left( -\frac{n \Delta_{i_\ell}^2}{16 i_\ell \log_2(k)} \right). \] where \(L = \log_2(k)\).

    Thomson Sampling - Bayesian Setting

    Thomson Sampling Algorithm

  • In round \( t \) ,Thompson sampling samples a bandit environment \( {\nu }_{t} \) from the posterior of \( Q \) given \( {A}_{1},{X}_{1},\ldots ,{A}_{t - 1},{X}_{t - 1} \) and then chooses the arm with the largest mean.

    • Theorem 36.1 Upper Bound of the Bayesian Regret

  • Let \( \left( {\mathcal{E},\mathfrak{B}\left( \mathcal{E}\right) ,Q,P}\right) \) be a \( k \) -armed Bayesian bandit environment such that for all \( \nu \in \mathcal{E} \) and \( i \in \left\lbrack k\right\rbrack \) ,the distribution \( {P}_{\nu i} \) is 1-subgaussian (after centering) with mean in \( \left\lbrack {0,1}\right\rbrack \) . Then the policy \( \pi \) of Thompson sampling satisfies \[{\mathrm{{BR}}}_{n}\left( {\pi ,Q}\right) \leq C\sqrt{{kn}\log \left( n\right) },\] where \( C > 0 \) is a universal constant.

    • Proof of Theorem 36.1

  • Define a clip function of the UCB: \[{U}_{t}\left( i\right) = {\operatorname{clip}}_{\left\lbrack 0,1\right\rbrack }\left( {{\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( {t - 1}\right) + \sqrt{\frac{2\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }{1 \vee {T}_{i}\left( {t - 1}\right) }}}\right) ,\]
  • Because of the definition of TS algorithm, \[\mathbb{P}\left( {{A}^{ * } = \cdot \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right) = \mathbb{P}\left( {{A}_{t} = \cdot \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right) \;\text{ a.s. } \tag{36.1}\]
  • Define an event \(E\) that for all \( t \in \left\lbrack n\right\rbrack \) and \( i \in \left\lbrack k\right\rbrack \) , \[\left| {{\widehat{\mu }}_{i}\left( {t - 1}\right) - {\mu }_{i}}\right| < \sqrt{\frac{2\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }{1 \vee {T}_{i}\left( {t - 1}\right) }}.\]
  • Bayesian regret is \[{\mathrm{{BR}}}_{n} = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\left( {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}}}\right) }\right\rbrack = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}} \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack }\right\rbrack \] and \[\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}} \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) + {U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}} \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack \] \[ = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {U}_{t}\left( {A}^{ * }\right) + {U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}} \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack \] \[ = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {U}_{t}\left( {A}^{ * }\right) \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack + \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}} \mid {\mathcal{F}}_{t - 1}}\right\rbrack .\] Then, \[{\mathrm{{BR}}}_{n} = \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\left( {{\mu }_{{A}^{ * }} - {U}_{t}\left( {A}^{ * }\right) }\right) + \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\left( {{U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}}}\right) }\right\rbrack . \tag{36.2}\]
  • Regret Decomposition:
    • 1. On the event \( {E}^{c} \) the terms inside the expectation are bounded by \( {2n} \)
    • 2. On the event \( E \) ,the first sum is negative and the second is bounded by \[\mathbb{I}\{ E\} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\left( {{U}_{t}\left( {A}_{t}\right) - {\mu }_{{A}_{t}}}\right) = \mathbb{I}\{ E\} \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {{A}_{t} = i}\right\} \left( {{U}_{t}\left( i\right) - {\mu }_{i}}\right) \] \[ \leq \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{t = 1}}^{n}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {{A}_{t} = i}\right\} \sqrt{\frac{8\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }{1 \vee {T}_{i}\left( {t - 1}\right) }} \leq \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}{\int }_{0}^{{T}_{i}\left( n\right) }\sqrt{\frac{8\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }{s}}{ds}\] \[ = \mathop{\sum }\limits_{{i = 1}}^{k}\sqrt{{32}{T}_{i}\left( n\right) \log \left( {1/\delta }\right) } \leq \sqrt{{32nk}\log \left( {1/\delta }\right) }.\]

    Thomson Sampling - Frequenist Analysis

    Follow-the-perturbed-leader Algorithm

  • Choose \( {F}_{1}\left( 1\right) ,\ldots ,{F}_{k}\left( 1\right) \) to be the cumulative distribution functions of the mean reward of each arm for a prior.
  • Let UPDATE be the function that updates the posterior for the played arm.

    • Theorem 36.2 Upper Bound of the Expected Number of Times Played

  • Assume that arm 1 is optimal. Let \( i > 1 \) be an action and \( \varepsilon \in \mathbb{R} \) be arbitrary. Then \[\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {{T}_{i}\left( n\right) }\right\rbrack \leq 1 + \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{s = 0}}^{{n - 1}}\left( {\frac{1}{{G}_{1s}} - 1}\right) }\right\rbrack + \mathbb{E}\left\lbrack {\mathop{\sum }\limits_{{s = 0}}^{{n - 1}}\mathbb{I}\left\{ {{G}_{is} > 1/n}\right\} }\right\rbrack , \tag{36.3}\] where \( {G}_{is} = 1 - {F}_{is}\left( {{\mu }_{1} - \varepsilon }\right) \) .

    • Proof of Theorem 36.2
